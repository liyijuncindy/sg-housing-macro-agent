# Engineering report: Singapore housing macroeconomic data agent

## Objective and delivered scope

This project implements a date-driven command-line workflow that identifies reviewed public predictor series, downloads official observations, validates and transforms them, selects a small indicator dashboard, and generates a report in the same run. It considers Singapore residential sales and rents, retaining separate private-price, private-rental and HDB-resale outcomes. The original task asks for useful predictor data and a reliable, reproducible product; it does not require training a production prediction model.

The submission includes source code, dependencies, representative raw and processed data, execution records, tests and sample reports. The [submission sample](../examples/english_submission_run/report.md) presents the reviewed English analysis with complete selected-series metadata. Its underlying observations and model execution come from the preserved [live GLM run](../examples/english_glm_v2_run/report.md). Presentation and editorial changes make no additional source or model calls. The [submission validation record](submission-validation.md) identifies the final verification and packaging results; [v2 validation](report-v2-validation.md) records the earlier live execution and independent numerical audit.

## Data selection and economic interpretation

The bounded catalogue contains sixteen explanatory candidates: resident and non-resident population, GDP, unemployment, individual employment income, aggregate disposable income, SORA, housing credit, private completed stock, vacancy, development pipeline, CPI, resident households, employed-household median income, total employment and HDB stock. The catalogue records exact series identities, units, coverage and reviewed mechanisms. Official keyword discovery is captured, but an unknown search result cannot silently become an approved series. This deliberately favours inspectable definitions over unrestricted autonomous discovery.

Each candidate must have finite, unique, parseable observations, adequate history, completeness and recency. Minimum histories are three annual, eight quarterly or twenty-four monthly observations; recent-window completeness must be at least seventy-five percent. The ten-year quality window prevents sparse early records from dominating a currently usable history. Full downloaded histories remain available for audit. Quality scores measure data usability, with disclosed engineering weights, rather than economic importance or predictive strength.

Rules mode prioritises quality and economic-family coverage with deterministic identifier tie-breaking. Agent mode inspects every candidate and combines the same eligibility gate with scope, mechanisms, coverage and exploratory correlations. Five is a configurable maximum, not a mandatory count or a proven optimal number. Family diversity is a preference, and useful complementary measures can share a family. Eligible exclusions are capacity or judgement decisions rather than failed quality checks.

The captured GLM dashboard selects resident unemployment, SORA, private vacant-unit count, resident households and median employment income among resident employed households. These provide labour-market, financing, absorption, household-demand and affordability context. Their limitations remain explicit: household growth is net stock change, vacant units need not be actively listed, and the income median excludes households without an employed member and includes employer CPF and bonus allocation. Public-housing stock is distinct from private supply. The full indicator pool explains all sixteen decisions and preserves potentially useful excluded alternatives.

## System design and agent responsibilities

Python's standard library handles HTTP requests, parsing, validation, calendar arithmetic, JSON/CSV storage and report generation. JSON dictionaries provide transparent module boundaries; immutable directories replace a database for this small auditable workflow. The optional OpenAI SDK supplies direct tool loops, including SiliconFlow Chat Completions for GLM. Matplotlib generates charts but is unnecessary for offline report replay. A command-line interface satisfies the task without a web application or scheduler.

The agent has three allowed actions: list captured candidates, inspect evaluated facts, and submit selected identifiers, individual reasons and qualitative narratives. It cannot execute code, invent download addresses or bypass eligibility. Evidence identifiers must belong to inspected series. Submission checks enforce known identifiers, the selection limit, synchronized decisions, complete reasons, permitted evidence and prose constraints. Model numbers do not enter report calculations; deterministic code inserts calculated values into the report.

Provider-specific credentials and fixed endpoints prevent accidental credential reuse across services. Keys remain local and outside Git and delivery archives. Model interactions have bounded turns and request timeouts; failures stay explicit rather than silently producing a rules-mode result. Rules mode remains an independently labelled baseline. Returned model identifiers, usage, tool calls, repairs and timings are recorded.

Every candidate is attempted independently through SingStat first. Transient requests receive bounded retries, followed by a later second candidate pass. Only then may the default policy use reviewed MOM/MAS backups for unemployment, individual employment income or SORA. Other candidates have no independent backup. The strict SingStat policy retains retries without fallbacks. Permanent schema or identity changes fail explicitly. A single maintenance page never establishes a whole-site outage or stops unrelated candidates.

## Dates, transformations and report evidence

The reporting date is an observation-reference cutoff using the captured data vintage. Observation dates, publication dates, table-update times and retrieval times remain separate. Documented June-end dates govern population and HDB stock; household counts conservatively use year-end where a consistent exact reference date was not verified. This does not assert that observations were measured or published on the cutoff day. Known publication dates are honoured, but historical availability is not reconstructed.

Calendar keys determine comparison baselines. Missing quarters cannot be replaced by neighbouring rows. Annual series retain annual comparisons; seasonality-sensitive GDP and employment-income series use year-on-year changes. Quantities and indices use percentage changes, unemployment changes use percentage points, and SORA changes use basis points. Rates are stored in percentage units. Missing baselines and zero denominators return unavailable results with reasons. There is no interpolation, forward filling or synthetic history.

The English report presents actual market movements, charts, selected signals, derived vacancy, empirical checks and the complete indicator pool. Each selected series includes its source link and identifier, definition and scope, unit, observation frequency, update-frequency statement, latest usable observation and date, calculated changes, possible sale/rent mechanisms, lag and limitations. Update cadence is distinguished from observation frequency and identified as unavailable or inferred where a verified release calendar is absent. Index levels are not currency prices. Private rental and stock coverage differ, including executive-condominium treatment.

Private vacancy is derived from matching-quarter vacant units divided by matching completed private stock, even when stock is excluded from the dashboard. Each calculation records its formula, dates, inputs and exact observations. Normalized records retain raw cell positions and hashes. Source-routing records preserve retries, fallbacks and bounded error-response evidence. A new live run creates a new directory; replay verifies saved hashes and regenerates the exact report without network or model calls. Explicit saved-source analysis is separately labelled and never pretends to be a fresh download. Historical presentation formats remain replayable.

## Exploratory empirical checks

Three housing indices are captured separately from the explanatory candidates. All forty-eight candidate/outcome pairs are retained, including skipped comparisons. Quarterly associations compare candidate year-on-year changes with outcome quarter-on-quarter growth. Monthly features use actual quarter-end months; annual associations remain separate against Q4 year-on-year outcomes. Predeclared leads are zero, one, two and four quarters, or years for annual series. At least eight nonconstant matched values are required. Statistical change endpoints start in 2015 to avoid spanning documented index-methodology transitions.

Supported quarterly pairs receive an expanding single-indicator ridge benchmark predicting next-quarter index growth. At least twenty-four training pairs and eight test predictions are required. Scaling and fitting use training rows only; future targets cannot enter training. Historical mean, last-quarter growth and zero growth use identical test dates. Errors, direction accuracy, sample windows and every prediction are saved. Annual candidates are not interpolated into quarterly prediction samples. Full methods are documented in [report v2](report-v2.md).

The selecting agent sees exploratory correlations, not held-out forecast errors. These checks neither optimise the selected combination nor provide an untouched confirmatory test. In the recorded run, twenty-four pairs supported prediction checks. Of the selected dashboard's nine supported pairs, only one single-indicator model beat all three baselines by mean absolute error. This supports cautious monitoring interpretation rather than a claim of robust forecasting ability.

## Verification and demonstrated improvements

The live GLM run captured all nineteen series from SingStat through forty-seven logical retrieval records, with no observed maintenance response, fallback or missing series. It preserved 2,816 source observations. Six model requests completed in approximately 225 seconds overall; an empty selection was rejected and repaired. Returned usage and published-price cost estimates are retained rather than treated as guaranteed future budgets.

Independent verification checked raw periods and values, changes using Decimal arithmetic, correlations using NumPy, ridge predictions using independent matrix solves, forecast chronology, metrics and matching-quarter vacancy. The audit, original execution and review inputs remain inspectable. Final test, isolated-install, replay and archive results belong to the dated submission validation record rather than an unsupported claim about future runs.

Nontrivial failures led to concrete corrections. An unfiltered CPI response reached the API cell limit; retrieval now requests exact series and rejects truncation. A missing-quarter regression demonstrates why row-position comparisons use the wrong baseline. Tests also cover missing or stale observations, zero denominators, reference-date handling, changed units/schema, fabricated evidence, failed model repairs, immutable runs and tampered replay files. Maintenance recognition distinguishes a current standalone outage page from normal content quoting an old announcement.

Syntax and evidence checks accepted some economically incorrect model prose. The earlier review corrected six reasons and three narrative fields. Review for the complete submission retains those edits and corrects additional vacancy-denominator, household-stock, income-coverage and timing wording: seven decision reasons and twelve narrative fields in total. Each edited field is labelled, while original decisions and trace remain preserved. Selected identifiers and statistical inputs are unchanged. Editorial review is a visible verification step, not proof that automated checks establish semantic correctness.

## Limitations and next two weeks

Latest-vintage data and incomplete historical publication records prevent a strict real-time backtest. Aggregate indices do not describe an individual property's price. Current comparisons are exploratory, with multiple comparisons, different sample windows and no causal identification. Data-usability gates and a bounded catalogue cannot establish an exhaustive or universally optimal predictor set. Automatic revision comparison and proactive schema monitoring are also unfinished.

During the first week, I would prioritise verified release calendars, archived revisions and explicit input-availability dates, then add source-revision diffs and tests that reject information unavailable at a forecast origin. During the second week, I would predefine a market and prediction horizon, compare selection approaches within training periods, test window stability and reserve an independent evaluation period. Any justified joint model would be compared with simple baselines under the same availability policy. These steps extend the existing outcome retrieval and retrospective checks rather than treating them as future work.

Further catalogue additions would require reviewed definitions and reliable sources, especially HDB transaction activity, completions and policy effects. Broader backups and semantic-review checks would improve robustness. Scheduling, notifications and a user interface would follow demonstrated unattended reliability; they are outside the required command-line submission.
